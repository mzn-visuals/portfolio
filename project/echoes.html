Echoes

A digital time capsule for your phone. Seal a moment — voice, photo, or message — and unlock it exactly one year later.

Overview

‍

Echoes is a personal project built for the love of the game — a time capsule app for Android. Capture a moment, lock it away, and meet it again exactly one year later. No cloud, no tracking, no peeking. Just you and who you used to be.

‍

  • Time-locked memories that unseal exactly 365 days after creation
  • Multiple content types — text, voice recordings, photos, and 360° photospheres
  • Location locking — optionally require returning to the exact spot to unlock

‍

Everything stays on-device. No accounts, no servers, no data leaving your phone. Smart notifications alert you when an echo is ready or when you're near a location-locked memory. An interactive map shows where your echoes are scattered across the world.

Stack

‍

Built natively for Android using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose — no cross-platform shortcuts. The UI is Material 3 with smooth animations throughout, designed to feel as considered as the concept behind it.

‍

  1. Kotlin + Jetpack Compose — fully native Android, Material 3 design system
  2. Room — local database keeping everything on-device, zero cloud dependency
  3. Google Maps SDK — interactive map showing where echoes are placed in the world

‍

Geofencing handles location-based unlock triggers via Android's location APIs, and WorkManager schedules notification delivery without draining battery. Requires Android 12.0 or higher. Camera and microphone recommended for the full experience.
