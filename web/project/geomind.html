GeoMind

Promotional video production and graphic design for a geospatial web application built around location-based data visualization and analysis.

Overview

‍

GeoMind is a geospatial web application built around location-based data visualization and analysis. As a freelance creative, I was brought on to handle the visual communication side of the project — translating a technically dense product into content that could speak to a broader audience. My scope covered two core deliverables: a promotional video edit and a poster graphic design, both managed independently from initial concept through to final handoff.

‍

  • Promotional video edit with narrative structure and pacing
  • Motion graphics and transitions using Adobe After Effects
  • Poster graphic design with vector work and typographic hierarchy

‍

Both deliverables were handled entirely independently. There was no creative team — concept, execution, revisions, and delivery were all self-managed. This kind of end-to-end ownership is something I bring to every project: the ability to take a brief, make strong creative decisions, and ship something that actually works without needing heavy direction.

Tools Used

‍

  1. Adobe Premiere Pro — Video editing and timeline assembly
  2. Adobe After Effects — Motion graphics and visual effects
  3. Adobe Illustrator — Poster design and vector graphics

‍

The promotional video involved conceptualizing the narrative structure and pacing from scratch, cutting and assembling footage, building motion graphics and transitions, applying color grading, and mixing and syncing audio. The poster design involved developing the visual concept independently, designing with clean vector work and strong typographic hierarchy, and delivering print and digital-ready exports.
